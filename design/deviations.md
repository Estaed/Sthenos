# Deliberate breaks from Tarik Base v6

- **Orange on done sets (2026-10-24, Tarık, round 5):** logged sets carry an orange tint, so a finished
  day's sheet is well over the base's ~2 % orange. On this screen orange means "done", the progress of
  the workout is the key fact; the current set uses a neutral ink ring instead of the orange one.
- **Orange to red on the muscle map (2026-10-26, Tarık):** a muscle's colour runs from pale orange to red with its
  weekly sets, so red, outside the base's one-orange palette, marks the most worked muscles. A heat scale asked for by
  name; the rest of the screen stays grey with orange for done sets.
- **Dark-theme ramp stops (2026-10-26, Task-06):** in dark the muscle map's orange is #EB5200 and its red #FF3B30, not the
  prototype's #FF5A00 and #E5262E: measured in OKLab, the prototype's red came back toward the body grey above 5 sets.
- **Bar on a running or finished day (2026-10-26):** "Week N" shows as "WN" and a finished day's Summary is an icon, so
  the muscle button keeps 44 px beside the clock at 360 px.
